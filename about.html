About Moonlight Accessories

Premium hair extensions for the modern woman — based in Lahore, delivered across Pakistan.

Our story

Moonlight Accessories began with a simple frustration: finding hair extensions in Pakistan that actually look like real hair. Too often what arrives is stiff, unnaturally shiny, and impossible to blend — and it never quite matches the shade in the photo.

So we built the shop we wanted to buy from. We select premium synthetic fibre that moves and catches the light the way real hair does, photograph every shade honestly, and deliver it to your door with nothing to pay until it arrives.

Caring for your extensions

Treated well, a good synthetic set lasts a long time:

  • Detangle gently from the ends upwards with a wide-tooth comb;
  • Wash sparingly in cool water with a mild shampoo, then air dry — never wring them out;
  • Keep heat low; synthetic fibre does not recover from being scorched the way human hair does;
  • Store them flat or hanging, out of direct sunlight.
